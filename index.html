What we do

Four pillars. One data platform.

From raw source systems to board-ready dashboards, Fathom covers the full data lifecycle with managed services built for scale, reliability, and speed.

Data Ingestion

Capture data from any source — databases, APIs, event streams, files — with schema-aware connectors that just work.

  • Batch & real-time streaming
  • 100+ pre-built connectors
  • Schema auto-discovery
  • Exactly-once delivery

Data Lake Management

Centralised, governed storage layers built on open table formats. Query your lake directly — no lock-in, no migration debt.

  • Iceberg / Delta / Hudi support
  • Time travel & rollback
  • Automated compaction
  • Cost-aware tiering

Data Pipelines

Reliable, observable ELT pipelines with built-in lineage. Define transforms as code; let Fathom handle scheduling, retries, and alerts.

  • DAG-based orchestration
  • SQL & Python transforms
  • Data lineage tracking
  • Failure alerts & replay

Analytics

From ad-hoc SQL exploration to executive dashboards. Fathom's semantic layer gives every team one source of truth.

  • Semantic metrics layer
  • BI-agnostic dashboards
  • Self-serve exploration
  • Embedded analytics API

Why Fathom

Built for teams who treat data as product.

We don't just hand you tools — we operate the platform with you, so your engineers can focus on building, not babysitting infrastructure.

01

Open by default

No proprietary formats, no lock-in. Your data lives in open table formats (Iceberg, Delta) on storage you own. Move engines anytime without migrating a single byte.

02

Operated, not just delivered

Fathom runs your pipelines in production — monitoring, on-call, SLAs included. You get the benefit of a platform team without hiring one.

03

Cost-aware engineering

Every query, every transform is cost-tracked. Automated tiering and compaction keep your cloud bill predictable as data volume grows.
